## 📊 Projects Overview

### 1. Sales Summary Dashboard (Power BI)
![Sales Dashboard]([TEMPEL LINK GAMBAR DASHBOARD DI SINI])
* **Objective:** Developing an interactive corporate sales center to track monthly and yearly target achievements by car models, regions, and sales teams.
* **Key Metrics:** Total Volume (2,908 sales), Total Sales by Model (Alphard, Avanza, Calya, Fortuner, Yaris Cross, Zenix), MoM Growth %, and Sales vs. Target variances.
* **Business Value:** Provides regional directors and supervisors with granular insights to monitor which models and regions are lagging behind quota so they can quickly adjust local marketing efforts.

### 2. Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
![Churn Dashboard]([TEMPEL LINK GAMBAR DASHBOARD DI SINI])
* **Objective:** Identifying critical churn drivers and behavioral patterns for a telecom/subscription business to minimize customer attrition.
* **Key Insights:** 
  * The business is facing a significant **26.86% Churn Rate** (out of 6,687 total customers, 1,796 churned).
  * Competitor actions (better offers/devices) and service dissatisfaction are the leading reasons for customer departures.
  * Customers with month-to-month contracts and high customer service calls (averaging 2.14 calls for churned vs. 0.41 for retained) carry the highest risk profile.
* **Recommendations:** Implement proactive retention offers before month-to-month contracts renew, and streamline customer service response times for accounts with repeat inquiries.

### 3. Executive Marketing & Sales Performance (Tableau)
![Executive Dashboard]([TEMPEL LINK GAMBAR DASHBOARD DI SINI])
* **Objective:** Synthesizing financial returns and acquisition performance across distinct marketing channels for executive review.
* **Key Insights:** 
  * Achieved **6.36M in Total Earnings** with a peak single-month performance of 1.45M.
  * Television Ads brought in the highest earnings (1.84M) and registrations, closely followed by Facebook Pages (1.33M).
  * YouTube Channels yielded the lowest revenue contribution (351K).
* **Recommendations:** Optimize marketing budget allocations by scaling up high-performing television and social media campaigns while auditing underperforming search/video ad placements.

### 4. Sales Command Center & E-Commerce Dashboard (Metabase)
![Operations Dashboard]([TEMPEL LINK GAMBAR DASHBOARD DI SINI])
* **Objective:** Monitoring real-time online product transactions, fulfillment status, and demography behavior patterns.
* **Key Metrics:** Total Orders (200), Total Sales Volume (Rp 236.27M), and category breakdowns highlighting dynamic everyday consumer product demand.
* **Business Value:** Empowers operational managers to optimize supply chain pipelines, manage inventory risk, and forecast category inventory shifts safely.
