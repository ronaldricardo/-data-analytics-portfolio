# Data Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my data analytics portfolio! This repository showcases interactive dashboards I designed across various industries, utilizing modern BI tools (**Power BI, Tableau, Metabase, Google Data Studio, and Spreadsheets**). Each project focuses on translating raw datasets into actionable business strategies and clear executive stories.

---

## 📊 Projects Overview

### 1. Executive Marketing & Sales Performance (Microsoft Excel)
![Project 1 Dashboard](KODE_GAMBAR_DARI_ISSUE_1_DI_SINI)
*   **Objective:** Synthesizing financial returns, seasonal order trends, and acquisition performance across marketing channels for executive review.
*   **Key Insights:** 
    *   Achieved **6.36M in Total Earnings** with a peak single-month performance of 1.45M, backed by a core user acquisition volume of **2,634 registered clients**.
    *   Television Ads brought in the highest earnings (1.84M) and registrations, closely followed by Facebook Pages (1.33M).
*   **Business Value:** Provides commercial teams with multi-layered views to track month-over-month performance variations and scale high-performing television/social media campaigns.

### 2. Supermarket Sales Performance Analytics (Google Data Studio / Looker Studio)
![Project 2 Dashboard](KODE_GAMBAR_DARI_ISSUE_2_DI_SINI)
*   **Objective:** Evaluating cross-regional retail transactions, profitability distribution, and basket size metrics.
*   **Key Metrics:** Total Sales (**$322.97K**), Total Profit (**$15.38K**), and Average Order Value (**$322.97**).
*   **Business Value:** Identifies historical transaction trends across key target cities (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago) to help regional supply chain managers align logistical bandwidth with demand distribution spikes.

### 3. Webinar & E-Book Sales Funnel (Google Spreadsheet)
![Project 3 Dashboard](KODE_GAMBAR_DARI_ISSUE_3_DI_SINI)

*   **Key Metrics:** Segmented revenue tracking (Rp 9,235,000 across 100 members for webinars vs. Rp 40,760,000 across 550 members for e-books).
*   **Business Value:** Evaluates product conversions by type and dynamic payment channel usage statistics, allowing growth teams to refine landing page conversion tactics.

### 4. Sales Command Center & E-Commerce Operations (Metabase)

### 6. Supermarket Sales & Inventory Matrix (Power BI)
![Project 6 Dashboard](KODE_GAMBAR_DARI_ISSUE_6_DI_SINI)

*   **Key Metrics:** Total Revenue (**$322.97K**), Total Profit (**$15.38K**), and Product Volumes (**5,510 items**).
*   **Business Value:** Correlates daily order frequencies with product lines to provide category managers with clear evidence for stock optimization and localized store layout structuring.

### 7. End-to-End Customer Churn Risk Analysis (Power BI)
![Project 7 Dashboard](KODE_GAMBAR_DARI_ISSUE_7_DI_SINI)
*   **Objective:** Identifying critical churn drivers and correlating customer service touchpoints with user attrition probability.
*   **Key Insights:** 
    *   The business faces a significant **26.86% Churn Rate** (out of 6,687 total customers, 1,796 churned), heavily driven by competitor actions.
    *   Customers with month-to-month contracts and high customer service calls (averaging 2.14 calls for churned vs. 0.41 for retained) carry the highest risk profile.
*   **Recommendations:** Implement proactive retention offers before month-to-month contracts renew, and build automated triggers when a client makes more than 2 service calls.

### 8. Atlas Labs HR Attrition Tracker (Power BI)

*   **Business Value:** Equips HR business partners with department-specific hiring timelines and role benchmarks to mitigate attrition risks and streamline proactive recruitment cycles.
